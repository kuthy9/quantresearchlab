## 2. Findings on the way (each fixed with its own failing test first)

**H3 — the LLM read `price_relations` the other way round.** The rows
were `{relation: above|inside|below, distance_atr}` from *price's* point
of view (`above` = price is above the object). Every real run — run 1, run
2, run 3 and the first launch of this backtest — read `above` as "the
object is above price", so for a SHORT it named an invalidation object
*below* price and a target *above* it, and the geometry check downgraded
the opportunity (`opportunity_incoherent`). Measured on the first launch
(five runs, ~1 h each, killed once the cause was clear; journals
`60588ef683f6fa02`, `dbaed4da8feb7691`, `10de0670891aea71`,
`709409d3b1b5c3b6`, `c2fa5c3607c09d95`):

| run | calls | opportunities proposed (not NONE) | `opportunity_incoherent` | survived |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| week, low | 129 | 26 | 24 | 1 |
| week, high | 64 | 14 | 14 | 0 |
| day (RTH 01-03), low | 114 | 27 | 27 | 0 |
| day, high | 48 | 15 | 15 | 0 |
| day, max | 11 | 6 | 6 | 0 |

86 of 88 proposals died on geometry. Every rejection reason in the sample
is the mirror image of a correct reading (for example SHORT with the stop
at a pool `above` = price above the pool = the pool *below* price). The
rows are now `{object_id, position: above_price | below_price |
contains_price, offset_atr}` — the *object's* place, offset signed the
same way — and the prompt defines them and restates the direction rule in
those terms. The second launch, on the new rows: 0 incoherent in 54 calls,
15 opportunities survived, and the first trades of the project were placed
(§4).

**H4 — a plan chosen while in a position was entered on the exit bar.**
Second launch, day-low: SHORT ×3 at 16375.5 (09:02 NY), stopped at
16382.75 one minute later; while the position was open the LLM had moved
its opportunity to a SHORT at the next pool (16382.5, the stop's own
level), and the machine — IDLE again on the exit bar — submitted it the
same minute, before any call had seen the outcome. A closed signature was
already blocked (found by the scenario tests: an unchanged plan was
resubmitted on the exit bar); now, after any close, the machine submits
nothing until the Brain has been called again with `last_outcome` in
front of it. Third launch started on this machine.

**M7, closed.** The simulator's margin model rejects a 5-contract order
when the gate sizes it against equity while the account's available funds
are below 5 × 20 000 — first seen once in the second launch, then fourteen
times in 80 minutes in the third (§8). The gate now caps the quantity by
`floor(available_funds / margin_per_contract)` (`risk.json` gained
`margin_per_contract`, 20 000) and vetoes `position_size` when that is
zero.

## 3. Method

- Window: warm-up 2021-12-27; the week emits 2022-01-02 18:00 → 01-07 17:00
  NY (6 900 bars); the day runs emit 2022-01-03 09:00 → 17:00 NY (480
  bars). `--broker sim`, `risk/configs/risk.json` (0.5 % risk, one
  position, RR ≥ 1.5, TTL 15 bars, max 5 contracts),
  `execution/configs/simulated_executor.json` (100 000, margin 20 000 per
  contract). The clock is the bar's `known_at` everywhere; the wall-clock
  guard test passes on the tree.
- Efforts: a probe of one journaled input (run 3, call 4) before
  scheduling — `low` 13.8 s / 2.5 k completion tokens, `high` 24.8 s /
  5.2 k, `max` 216 s / 44 k (42.7 k reasoning), and `max` overran the
  32 768 `max_tokens` (empty reply) so it runs from
  `brain/configs/main_brain_max.json` (65 536, 900 s). A week at `max`
  would take about 100 hours; the efforts are compared on the identical
  RTH day (three standalone runs from the same warm-up and the same SLEEP
  state) and the week runs at `low` and `high`. Five runs, concurrent,
  staggered by two minutes (the Eye warm-up is CPU; the rest is waiting
  on DeepSeek). Under five concurrent runs a `low` call took ~30 s and a
  `high` call ~60 s.
- Costs use DeepSeek's published `deepseek-flash` rates
  (`brain/configs/llm_pricing.json`: peak 0.006 / 0.3 / 1.2 USD per
  million cache-hit / cache-miss / output tokens; off-peak half), both
  bounds reported.

Sharp-move recognition is the controller's and is identical across

evidence. `max` costs 6 × the latency and 4 × the tokens of `low` for
nothing measurable in 53 minutes and needs 65 k output tokens. Verdict by
the request's rule (precision first, cost second): keep `high` — the only
effort with a precision signal (confidence, survival, one target hit) —
and set it explicitly in `main_brain.json`; `low` is not adopted, `max`
is rejected.

Every scenario of the request that the LLM's decisions produced ran

Two things the tape shows about the *parameters*: (1) the RR floor
alone admits stops of 2–10 points (the LLM names the nearest pool as the
invalidation), which are hit within one to four bars on nearly every

distance in ATR would change most of these trades and is the first
parameter to test on the rerun; (2) with such stops the risk budget sizes
5 contracts, and with 100 000 of cash 5 × 20 000 of margin is not
available after the first loss — the week-high run submitted fourteen
5-contract orders in 80 minutes that the executor rejected one after the
other, each with a slightly different signature so the per-signature
block did not bite. The gate now caps the quantity by the available
funds at `margin_per_contract` (M7 closed); the finished day journals
replay unchanged under the new gate (no order in them exceeded the cap).

| controller | 481 | 0.02 ms | 0.03 ms | 0.05 ms | 0.01 s |

| reduce | 153 | 1.0 ms | 1.9 ms | 7.7 ms | 0.2 s |

| plan + gate + machine | 481 | 0.01 ms | 0.6 ms | 3.5 ms | 0.01 s |
| bar end to end (emitted bars) | 481 | 1.6 ms | 44 s (82) | 98 s (173) | 4 889 s (5 931) |

A bar without an LLM call costs 1.6 ms end to end; the Eye adds 16 ms.
The LLM call is 99.9 % of the wall time: 4 885 of 4 889 seconds. So the
system's response speed is the LLM's latency (30 s at `low`, 53 s at
`high` for the median call, 2–3 minutes at the tail) times the number of
calls. In live trading the decision for a 1m bar therefore lands 30–60 s
after the bar closes, inside the next bar; every other component is below
the tick size of the clock. Levers, in order of leverage: fewer calls
(23 % of the day-low calls changed nothing; `relation_change` of a
watched object was the single largest trigger at 105 of 469 reasons — a
trigger confined to the opportunity's own objects instead of the whole
watch list is the first thing to measure), a shorter input (median 20 k
characters; the bounded evidence lists already halved it), and the
effort itself (`low` halves the latency at the precision cost of §7).
None of these was changed here: the first two alter what the Brain sees
and need the rerun to be judged.

## 10. Regression baseline

`regression_baselines.json` freezes the two complete day runs
(`302c54bb21d9d77e`, `04e7302f3cb72bbd`) with their summaries;
`brain/tests/test_regression_baseline.py` (`research_orchestration`)
replays each with the current controller, Brain, plan builder, gate and
executor and requires the replay to reproduce and the fresh summary to
equal the frozen one on every deterministic field. Run it after any change
to those components:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m pytest brain/tests/test_regression_baseline.py -m research_orchestration -o addopts='' -p no:cacheprovider
```

The week runs join the file when the rerun completes.
