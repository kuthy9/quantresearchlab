# Eye event edge over 2023 — pre-registration

*2026-09-22. Written before any 2023 outcome was computed. The Eye runs
alone (no Brain, no LLM, no Risk, no execution); the study asks whether the
SMC events the Eye already emits carry a repeatable statistical edge on their
own. It is descriptive research, not a strategy backtest.*

## 0. Governance

- 2023 is the `brain_validation` window of `configs/data_splits.json` (made
  for the retired typed Brain's mappings). Scanning it for edges consumes it
  as an out-of-sample year for anything this study selects. The user chose
  2023; the mitigation is this pre-registration, a first-half / second-half
  split inside 2023, and leaving 2024 onward untouched: a candidate found here
  is confirmed only on the `rolling_oof` window (2024-02 → 2026-03), never on
  the sealed holdout.
- No 2024 price is read: the tape is loaded up to 2024-01-01 00:00 New York;
  forward windows that would need later bars are truncated and dropped.
- No parameter below is tuned on outcomes. Every threshold is fixed here; the
  report lists how many cells were tested.

## 1. Recording (outcome-blind): `eyes/scripts/scan_sparse_events.py`

- The registered Eye (`shares/core/eye_factory.build_eye`, `configs/model.json`)
  over `data/processed/nq_1m_previous_session_front_v2_3_2017_2026.parquet`,
  warm-up from 2022-12-01, recording from 2023-01-01 to 2024-01-01 (New York).
  The pass is checkpointed: every 5,000 bars (and at each month change) the
  buffered rows are written as parquet parts, the Eye is pickled and the
  manifest commits the parts; a pass that dies records the failure and
  `--resume` continues from the last checkpoint.  The Eye's audit journal
  lives beside the checkpoint (`<out>/_state/journal`).  Recording rules are
  unchanged by this; only the committed parts are data.
- **Event rows** (sparse): every event of the kinds `mss_core_confirmed`,
  `qualified_bos`, `displacement_observed`, `sweep_confirmed`,
  `acceptance_confirmed`, `structure_direction_confirmed`, `level_reached`
  on 5m / 15m / 1H / 4H, plus 1m `sweep_confirmed` / `acceptance_confirmed`
  whose swept level's own scale is 15m / 1H / 4H (the level's scale is taken
  from the `source_timeframe` the Eye published for that `level_id`).
- **Bar rows**: every completed 5m / 15m / 1H / 4H bar (the matched baseline
  and the active-leg flips).
- Each row carries the snapshot context at that bar: per scale (4H, 1H, 15m,
  5m) external / internal direction, delivery phase, active / last leg
  direction, displacement direction and age, protected swing intact, dealing
  range location, ATR; the session name / phase; the 1m close and ATR.

## 2. Event families (the hypotheses)

The primary scale is **15m**; 5m, 1H and 4H are run the same way for
comparison. 1H / 4H enter the 15m study only as context strata, never as a
direction. Direction is the event's own (`long` / `short`); for a sweep it is
the reversal direction the Eye assigns.

| id | family | row filter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | MSS | `mss_core_confirmed` |
| F2 | BOS | `qualified_bos` |
| F3 | DISP | `displacement_observed`, lifecycle `active` (started / exhausted reported descriptively) |
| F4 | SWEEP | `sweep_confirmed` on the scale (a break of that scale's swing that closed back inside) |
| F5 | ACCEPT | `acceptance_confirmed` on the scale (the break held) |
| F6 | SDC | `structure_direction_confirmed` |
| F7 | LEGFLIP | the scale's `active_leg_direction` changed between two consecutive closes of that scale |
| F8 | HTF-SWEEP | 1m `sweep_confirmed` of a 15m / 1H / 4H level, grouped by the level's scale |

Simple combinations (15m only):

| id | combination |
| --- | --- |
| C1 | F1 with `displacement_context_present` |
| C2 | F1 with a `prior_sweep` |
| C3 | F1 within 60 minutes after an F4 of the same direction on the same scale |

## 3. Outcomes

- Entry: the close of the 1m bar at the event's `known_at` (the first price a
  consumer could act on).
- Horizons: 15, 30, 60, 120, 240 minutes of clock time. A horizon whose window
  is missing real 1m bars (a session break, a weekend, a halt) is dropped for
  that event; the count is reported.
- Per horizon: signed return (close at the horizon), MFE and MAE (1m highs /
  lows inside the window), all in points and in the event's 15m ATR (`R15`).
- Target-before-stop within 240 minutes, two fixed brackets in 15m ATRs:
  1:1 (+1 / −1) and 2:1 (+2 / −1). A 1m bar touching both counts as the stop;
  a gap ends the window unresolved.
- **Matched baseline**: every completed 15m bar of 2023, both directions,
  same outcomes. An event's excess is its outcome minus the baseline mean for
  the same New York hour and direction. The 2023 up-drift is removed this way.

## 4. Strata

- **Day type (ex-post, descriptive only)** on the Regular Trading Hours
  (09:30–16:00) of the event's trade date (the 18:00 Globex open belongs to
  the next date). O, H, L, C the session's open / high / low / close, R = H − L,
  E1 the extreme reached first:
  - Reversal: |E1 − O| ≥ 0.35 R and C lies on the other side of O from E1 with
    |C − O| ≥ 0.25 R;
  - Trend (not Reversal): |C − O| ≥ 0.5 R and C in the 25 % of the range at the
    end the day moved to;
  - Chop: otherwise.
  It uses the day's own future, so it describes where an edge lives; it is
  not a filter a trader could apply.
- **Higher-scale context (causal)**: the event direction against the 1H and
  4H external direction at the event — `with` (both agree), `against` (both
  disagree), `mixed` (anything else, including none).
- **Range context (causal)**: inside an active 15m dealing range or not.
- Session: Regular Trading Hours or overnight.

## 5. Statistics and the candidate-edge rule

- Mean and median of each outcome; standard errors clustered by trade date;
  hit rate; the median MFE / MAE ratio; bracket win rates against the
  baseline's.
- Halves: H1 = January–June, H2 = July–December. Months: twelve.
- Primary horizon **60 minutes**; the others are secondary.
- A family × scale cell (or a combination) is a **candidate edge** when all of:
  1. n ≥ 150 over the year and n ≥ 60 in each half;
  2. the year's mean excess at 60 minutes (return, or MFE − MAE, or the 1:1
     bracket win rate) is positive with a day-clustered t ≥ 3.0 (about 32
     primary cells; Bonferroni at a 10 % family-wise rate);
  3. the same sign in H1 and H2, each with t ≥ 1.5;
  4. the same sign in at least 8 of 12 months;
  5. the mean excess return exceeds 1.0 point (a round trip's cost) — for the
     asymmetry measures, reported beside it.
- Everything else is reported as it is, with n.
