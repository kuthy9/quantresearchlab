### The two flagged fades

- **ACCEPT 15m, day_type = chop, win11 (t −3.16): withdrawn.** Day type is
  ex-post (pre-registration §4) and leaks the outcome: a placebo with no Eye
  event (each 15m close traded with its prior 60-minute move) passes the full
  rule on day_type = trend (t 3.37–3.43, halves 2.77 / 2.17). Keeping the
  first event per day takes this fade to t −0.11.
- **ACCEPT 1H, direction = short, ret60 (−0.331 R, t −3.55, n 238): the one
  rule worth an ex-ante test, with a weak prior.** Knowable at entry. In 2023
  it survives one-event-per-day (t −3.43), dropping the 5 best and worst days
  (t −4.01), 1 % winsorizing, next-bar-open and 1–5 minute delayed entries
  (t 3.4–3.8), a 1 pt cost (fade side +8.4 pts, t 3.56) and matching on the
  prior 60 / 240-minute move; 10 of 12 months negative. Against it:
  Westfall-Young p 0.31 over the full grid, H1 t −1.67 only, Q4 carries most
  of it (Q1–Q3 each |t| < 1.9), and sibling 1H short events go the other way
  (BOS 1H short +0.575 R, t 2.45; DISP_started 1H short +0.271 R, t 2.22).

## Limits and deviations

- **The Eye's external direction sticks.** `ext_15m` was short on every one
  of 2,981 15m bars from 2023-10-26 to 12-11 while NQ rose from 14,321 to
  16,246, and short on 64.5 % of 2023's 15m bars. Several long runs end only
  at a contract roll, where the Eye clears its history. A qualified BOS is by
  definition aligned with the external direction, so the BOS family and the
  htf strata (built from `ext_1H` / `ext_4H`) measure this state machine, not
  a correctly tracked BOS. Their null says nothing about the concept.
- **Roll resets:** the Eye clears its history at each quarterly roll
  (2023-03-13, 06-12, 09-11, 12-11); `ext_4H` is unset for 47–148 hours after
  each, and 4.6 % of rows fall within 72 hours of a roll.
- **Combinations:** C1 is empty on 15m (`displacement_context_present` is
  set only on 5m MSS, 484 of 2,505); C2 is empty (the Eye hard-codes the MSS
  `prior_sweep` evidence to `None`); C3 has 118 events (114 with a
  60-minute outcome), below the n ≥ 150 rule.
- **Power:** "no candidate" rules out only large effects. The minimum
  detectable effect at t = 3 is 0.05–0.23 R; most 15m CIs above still admit a
  1–4 pt cost-surviving effect in one direction.
- **Scope:** zones, FVG / OB interactions and range-auction events were not
  studied; `level_reached` is recorded but unused. Outcomes are a market
  entry at the event close with ATR brackets inside 240 minutes; no limit
  entry, structural stop / target or multi-session hold.
- **Deviations from the pre-registration:** the flags ran on every stratum
  cell, the ex-post day_type strata included (the pre-registration defines
  the rule for family × scale cells only, and no fade rule); the 51 filled
  no-trade minutes count as bars, where §3 drops windows missing real bars —
  under the strict reading the ex-post cell ACCEPT 15m day_type = trend win11
  moves from t 2.90 to 3.03 (a placebo passes that stratum too); same-bar
  de-duplication removes about 38 % of ACCEPT rows.
