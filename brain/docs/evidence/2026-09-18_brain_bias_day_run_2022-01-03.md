## 3. The bias as the Brain wrote it

38 segments, 28 changes, median segment 18 minutes. Bias scale by state
revision: 15m 206, 1H 58, 5m 18, 4H 15. The sequence around the overnight
low shows the mechanism:

| time | bias | basis (abridged) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 03:55 | SHORT/1H | 1H live short — active leg −1.08 ATR with a short BOS as its latest event |
| 04:05 | SHORT/15m | 15m −1.02 ATR with an age-0 short displacement; the 1H leg has eased |
| 04:15 | NEUTRAL | no scale at the 15m or above is live — the 15m leg is only −0.435 ATR |
| 04:20 | SHORT/15m | the 15m active leg is now short at −1.17 ATR with a 1-bar-old displacement |
| 04:35 | NEUTRAL | the 15m active leg is +0.91 ATR (under one ATR) |
| 04:45 | LONG/15m | the 15m active leg is +1.10 ATR with an age-0 long displacement |
| 07:00 | LONG/1H | the 1H is live in its own right — structure break and MSS core, +2.32 ATR |

Four changes in fifty minutes, each one the forming leg re-crossing ±1
ATR. The same pattern repeats at 08:16–08:45, 09:38–10:47 and 15:42–16:15.
Every one of the 26 directional changes named a structure direction of its
scale (internal, external or reset) that agreed with it — the model was
not ignoring structure; the flip is the threshold. Only 8 of the 26
directional segments agreed with the drift of the tape over their own
length.

The 5m set the bias 18 times (19:30 LONG, 20:45 SHORT, 21:35 LONG, 01:11
SHORT), each on a 5m leg past one ATR; T3 LONG (−17.5) was entered under
the 19:30 reading. The prompt names the 15m as the floor; the contract
allowed the 5m.

## 6. Diagnosis and the amendment (run B′)

The "live delivery" rule is a threshold on a continuous quantity
(`forming_leg_atr` beyond ±1) with nothing to hold it: the 15m forming leg
crosses one 15m ATR as a matter of course, so the 15m bias flaps between a
side and NEUTRAL, and each NEUTRAL drops the opportunity. The design's
intent — a bias that follows the scale that is delivering — is met; the
whipsaw is the absence of hysteresis, the standard defect of a threshold
rule, not a property of this day. Two minimal changes (spec §2.2, §2.6):

1. **Contract**: `bias.scale` ∈ `4H | 1H | 15m` (`BIAS_SCALES`); the 5m
   is an execution scale. Removes a state the design never wanted.
2. **Prompt**: a scale that went live *stays* live while its active leg
   keeps its sign; it stops being live when that leg ends (the active leg
   flips) or a structural event on that scale goes against it — not when
   the excursion shrinks under one ATR. The bias is carried forward until
   then. The thresholds themselves (one ATR, three bars), rule 4b and the
   debounce are unchanged.

Run B′ repeats the frozen window with only these two changes; the gate
for the execution layer is B′'s receipt. Expected: fewer bias changes and
NEUTRAL revisions, direction accuracy at least held, calls no higher.

## 9. Verdict on the layer

The bias mechanism is built and closed: the reply and the state carry it,
rule 4b enforces it (never once needed), the journal records every change
with its basis, the debounce and the bookkeeping kinds cut the calls
without losing coverage, and the hysteresis halved the whipsaw. The
reading itself is the model's and stays wrong more often than right on
this day at the segment level under both prompt variants; a third prompt
variant on the same tape would be fitting the 3rd, which the brief
forbids, so the layer stops here. The two execution facts above are the
execution layer's: the second is deterministic and consistent across four
runs and enters spec §3 as the bias-reversal exit; the first is recorded
for the entry rule and not tuned now.
