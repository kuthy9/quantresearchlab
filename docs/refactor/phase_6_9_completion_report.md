Bayesian-shaped Belief Updater, obstacle-aware DOL ranking, multi-path DOL
probability with explicit no-target mass, Signal Policy, and Trade Intent
projection. Evidence admission is exact-source, common-horizon, and replay
deterministic. Path protocol v1.2 additionally treats `correlation_key` as one
global dependency cluster: a fitted model cannot multiply cross-family facts

expired. The compatibility Brain also still receives the retained

## Playbook decision

Do not physically delete the three typed playbooks:

- DFP and LSR remain causal setup and entry-episode generators, including their
  invalidation, rearm, and managed-position contracts. They no longer own the
  market-path probability model.
- FAVR remains registered but parked/research-only because mature-range/value
  natural authority is still sparse.
- Phase 7 owns the normalized path set: continuation, deeper retracement,
  reversal, balance, failed breakout, and residual unknown.

This is the smallest safe decoupling: it removes duplicate probability/action
authority without discarding established replay and position-management
semantics.
